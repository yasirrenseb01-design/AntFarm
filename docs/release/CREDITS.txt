Research the ants are based on
  The game's biology is modelled on these published works (the full list, with links and what each one is used for, is
  kept with the game's source). Where the research does not say, the game's values are its own estimates.

  Walking and climbing
  - Zollikofer C.P.E. (1994) Stepping patterns in ants I-III. J Exp Biol 192:95-127
  - Reinhardt L., Blickhan R. (2014) Level locomotion in wood ants: evidence for grounded running. J Exp Biol 217:2358-2370
  - Wahl V. et al. (2015) Walking and running in the desert ant Cataglyphis fortis. J Comp Physiol A 201
  - Endlein T., Federle W. (2015) On heels and toes: how ants climb with adhesive pads and tarsal friction hair arrays.
    PLoS ONE 10(11)

  The nest and digging
  - Rasse P., Deneubourg J.-L. (2001) Dynamics of nest excavation and nest size regulation of Lasius niger.
    J Insect Behav 14:433-449
  - Buhl J. et al. (2004) Self-organized digging activity in ant colonies
  - Khuong A. et al. (2016) Stigmergic construction and topochemical information shape ant nest architecture.
    PNAS 113(5):1303-1308
  - Wang D., McSweeney K., Lowery B., Norman J.M. (1995) Nest structure of ant Lasius neoniger Emery and its implications
    to soil modification. Geoderma 66
  - Franks N.R., Sendova-Franks A.B. (1992) Brood sorting by ants: distributing the workload over the work-surface.
    Behav Ecol Sociobiol 30:109-123

  Food, sharing and work in the colony
  - Buffin A., Mailleux A.-C., Detrain C., Deneubourg J.-L. (2011) Trophallaxis in Lasius niger: a variable frequency and
    constant duration for three food types. Insectes Soc 58:177-183
  - Quque M. et al. (2019) A spatiotemporal analysis of the food dissemination process and the trophallactic network in the
    ant Lasius niger. Sci Rep
  - Quque M. et al. (2021) Hierarchical networks of food exchange in the black garden ant Lasius niger. Insect Sci
  - Mailleux A.-C., Deneubourg J.-L., Detrain C. (2009) Food transport in ants: do Lasius niger foragers maximize their
    individual load? C R Biol 332:500-506
  - Traniello J.F.A. (1983) Social organization and foraging success in Lasius neoniger: behavioral and ecological aspects
    of recruitment communication. Oecologia 59:94-100
  - Charbonneau D., Dornhaus A. et al. (2015) Workers 'specialized' on inactivity. Behav Ecol Sociobiol
  - Charbonneau D., Hillis N., Dornhaus A. (2015) 'Lazy' in nature: ant colony time budgets show high 'inactivity' in the
    field as well as in the lab. Insectes Soc
  - Mersch D.P., Crespi A., Keller L. (2013) Tracking individuals shows spatial fidelity is a key regulator of ant social
    organization. Science 340:1090-1093

  The queen
  - Fjerdingstad E.J., Keller L. (2004) Relationships between phenotype, mating behavior, and fitness of queens in the ant
    Lasius niger. Evolution 58
  - Holman L. et al. (2010) Identification of an ant queen pheromone regulating worker sterility

  Predators and defence
  - Brown C.A., Formanowicz D.R. (2012) The effect of leg autotomy on terrestrial and aquatic locomotion in the wolf spider
    Pardosa valens. J Arachnol 40:234-239
  - Villaseñor-Amador D. et al. (2023) Locomotor behaviour of tropical wolf spiders is affected by external temperature and
    body size, not load bearing. Animal Behaviour
  - Dejean A. et al. (2010) Arboreal ants use the "Velcro principle" to capture very large prey. PLoS ONE 5:e11331

  Reference photos sent by the maker, and the experience of ant keepers' guides and forums, filled in the rest.
