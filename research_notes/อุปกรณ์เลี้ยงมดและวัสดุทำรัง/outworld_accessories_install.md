## Outworld / arena: containers, size, lids, substrates, plants, clean-up crews

### Takeaway
No source gives a real outworld-size-per-colony rule; only product sizes and single setups exist. The firmest advice is to keep the outworld dry and shallow or bare so the colony does not move in, and that lids trade escape security against humidity, mould and barrier failure.

### Cited findings

**Container types and real sizes**
- Purpose: a second container, connected to or surrounding the nest, for food and waste. No dimensions given. [vendor-neutral encyclopaedia, not a keeper or peer source; general] [F] — [dewiki Formicarium](https://dewiki.de/Lexikon/Formicarium)
- Product sold as "Large": 10 x 20 x 10 cm, two connection ports, dual lid, "micro holes" for ventilation. No colony size stated. [vendor; species not stated] [F] — [Ant-Shack kit](https://www.ant-shack.com/products/large-ant-outworld-kit-nature)
- Product: Ytong nest 40 x 20 x 5 cm with chambers 20 mm deep, arena 20 x 20 cm on the glass plate, arena frame and cover. Not suitable for size-S ants because block edges have small gaps; suited to sizes M-L. Colony size not stated. [vendor; general] [F] — [AntCube](https://antcube.shop/en/product/ytongnest-40x20-with-arena/)
- A real *L. niger* progression: 20 x 10 tank with one test tube, then for about 200 workers a 30 x 20 x 20 tank with a 10 mm bore hole and an oil frame, attached to a 25 x 20 Ytong nest (units not written; presumably cm). [single keeper; *L. niger*] [F] — [Pierre, ameisenhaltung.de](https://www.ameisenhaltung.de/cafe/thread/8406-pierre-s-lasius-niger/)
- Test-tube-as-outworld: a kit uses a tube as the first food and waste area for queen plus first workers, with extra tubes 16 x 150 mm. [vendor; *L. niger*] [F] — [QualityAnts](https://qualityants.nl/en/starting-an-ant-colony-lasius-niger)
- A keeper reports that a large nest plus big outworld for about 10 *L. niger* workers led to rubbish being dumped in the nest. [single keeper; *L. niger*] [S] — [antstore thread (results set)](https://www.antstore.net/forum/post102393.html); the page read directly did not contain this post's detail, so the exact thread is unconfirmed.
- Nest size note: a 400 cm2 Ytong nest can hold thousands of *L. niger* workers; size should follow colony size. [single keeper; *L. niger*] [F] — [Markus, antstore](https://www.antstore.net/forum/topic16516.html)

**Lids and ventilation**
- Going lidless is advised because lids raise humidity, which degrades PTFE barriers; a plastic box lid that did not fit tightly also let *Tetramorium* out. [ผู้เลี้ยง-consensus (ANTdrew; drtrmiller is vendor); *Tetramorium*] [F] — [Formiculture 14693](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14693-best-way-to-contain-small-ants-like-tetramorium/)
- Same recommendation for the setup after a move: barrier and no lid, to avoid mould. [single keeper dermy; *T. immigrans*] [F] — [Formiculture 12181](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/12181-please-i-need-help-moving-my-ant-colony/)
- *L. niger* mould thread: SteveUK28 leaves lids off and uses Vaseline; if a lid is needed it has many holes, and he has used small computer fans for air. [single keeper; *L. niger* thread] [F] — [antstore mould thread](https://www.antstore.net/forum/post115893.html)
- Holes need coarse filter wadding or a grid fine enough to stop the ants; no mesh size given. [encyclopaedia; general] [F] — [dewiki](https://dewiki.de/Lexikon/Formicarium)
- Aquarium mesh lids: stock holes are too big; zero-gap inset lid proposed, feed through the mesh without opening, and coat a lip under the lid with Fluon in dry conditions for when the lid is off. No micron figure given. [vendor drtrmiller; species not stated] [F] — [Formiculture 2558](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/2558-fish-tank-mesh-lid)
- Disagreement: vendors sell lidded outworlds with micro holes ([Ant-Shack](https://www.ant-shack.com/products/large-ant-outworld-kit-nature)) while forum keepers advise lidless with a barrier ([14693](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14693-best-way-to-contain-small-ants-like-tetramorium/)). One vendor also suggests an airtight tub with desiccant packets, opened every couple of days, so Fluon survives in about 90% RH ([Formiculture 6620](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/6620-what-barriers-do-i-use-for-high-humidity/)).

**Substrates and keeping ants from nesting in the outworld**
- Keep the outworld as dry as possible; humidity may encourage digging. No more than about one inch of substrate (about 25 mm). [ผู้เลี้ยง-consensus on dry; [single keeper] on the 1-inch figure; *Pheidole* thread] [F] — [Formiculture 1690](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/1690-depth-of-substrate-in-outworld/)
- Plaster/grout/hydrostone floor with sand glued on top so ants cannot dig. Another keeper rejected this because it permanently spoils the glass tank. [single keeper each; disagreement; *Pheidole* thread] [F] — [Formiculture 1690](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/1690-depth-of-substrate-in-outworld/)
- A *Myrmica* queen left her tube and dug into a moss terrarium outworld; within a week soil and dead springtails were everywhere, mould grew on them, and she had to be removed. Replies: bare or non-diggable outworld is the practical fix; there is little to do in a naturalistic setup. [ผู้เลี้ยง-consensus; *Myrmica*] [F] — [Formiculture 7828](https://formiculture.com/topic/7828-how-to-prevent-ants-from-moving-into-outworld)
- A bare area with at most a water tube is also easier to clean. [single keeper dermy; species not stated] [F] — [Formiculture 7828](https://formiculture.com/topic/7828-how-to-prevent-ants-from-moving-into-outworld)
- *L. niger* arena with white sand-clay mix, a cactus and plants (the same mix sealed Ytong pores). [single keeper; *L. niger*] [F] — [Pierre](https://www.ameisenhaltung.de/cafe/thread/8406-pierre-s-lasius-niger/)
- Mould of about 50 x 70 mm grew in sand near the straw after a couple of months in a small *L. niger* setup; blamed on condensation; suggested top layer bone dry, lower layer slightly damp, air pump or small lamp, watering roughly once per 4 months. [single keeper; *L. niger*] [F] — [antstore mould thread](https://www.antstore.net/forum/post115893.html)
- A retailer says its outworld substrate does not absorb or retain humidity, which keeps the area dry so ants won't nest or dig there. [vendor; general] [S] — [Ant-Shack outworld (results set, exact page unconfirmed)](https://ant-shack.com/products/large-ant-outworld-ki-moai)
- Leaf litter: not found as an outworld substrate in any page read.

**Plants and decoration**
- A plant leaf that touches the wall bridges the barrier: a houseplant leaf defeated a vinegar-and-water backup tray. Concern about growing plants raised for a Fluon tank. [ผู้เลี้ยง-consensus; *Tetramorium* and unspecified] [F] — [Formiculture 14693](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14693-best-way-to-contain-small-ants-like-tetramorium/); [Formiculture 6468](https://formiculture.com/topic/6468-is-byformica-fluon-water-resistant)
- A formicarium touching the outworld nearly let *S. xyloni* breach a second colony. [single keeper; *Solenopsis xyloni*] [F] — [Formiculture 5405](https://formiculture.com/topic/5405-barrier-question)

**Clean-up crew**
- Springtails: added by scooping culture soil into the outworld (*Crematogaster*) or with coconut fibre; both keepers report success, nobody reports die-offs in that thread. Entomobryomorpha do well in bare nests; Poduromorpha need some substrate; avoid large clumps. A clump fell beside an entrance and the ants carried it in. Adding substrate to a 1,200-1,500 ant colony setup would be disruptive. [ผู้เลี้ยง-consensus on working; [single keeper] on the group split; *Crematogaster*, others] [F] — [Formiculture 12923](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/12923-adding-springtails-to-tha-formicariums/)
- Springtails do not guarantee a clean setup: mould grew on dead springtails in the moss terrarium after the queen dug it up. [single keeper; *Myrmica*] [F] — [Formiculture 7828](https://formiculture.com/topic/7828-how-to-prevent-ants-from-moving-into-outworld)
- Search excerpts only: a *Camponotus* colony's springtails ate all the mould; one colony feasted on them at first; one keeper saw them near brood but no egg eating; some may escape. [single keepers; mixed species] [S] — [Formiculture results set: 4065](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/4065-springtails/), [2627](https://formiculture.com/topic/2627-methods-for-introducing-springtails)
- Isopods: in a bioactive carpenter-ant outworld, ants chased isopods off jelly; roly-poly types froze to pass; isopods ate leftovers. No predation described. [vendor video AntsCanada; *Camponotus*] [F, transcript page] — [Ants vs Isopods](https://rosetta.to/u/antscanada/ants-vs-isopods-war-or-truce?t=0)

### Inferences
- For a game: a dry shallow arena is the low-effort stable state; moss, plants and damp soil add risk of colony relocation, mould and barrier bridges. This is drawn from the cited threads, none of which measured it.
- A sensible size mechanic is a soft penalty for "too large too early" and "too small", not a hard square-cm rule, since no figure exists.

### Gaps
- Outworld area per worker: not found. Leaf litter use: not found. Isopod or springtail results with *L. niger*: not found. Mesh aperture for *L. niger*: not found in a page read; a tea-strainer excerpt and a 100-micron excerpt were seen in search only, without a confirmed thread.

## Connections: tubing, connectors, drilling, sealing, plugs, several nests, opening a new nest

### Takeaway
There is no standard; keepers use a snug friction fit of vinyl tube and step-down joints. Reported sizes run from about 10 mm bore to 16-20 mm nominal, and posts often do not say whether a figure is inner or outer diameter. New nests are opened by attaching and waiting; *L. niger* may take weeks and forcing is discouraged.

### Cited findings

**Sizes and material**
- Vinyl sizes OD/ID in inches: 7/8-3/4, 3/4-1/2, 1/2-3/8, 3/8-1/4, 1/4-1/8. Uses 3/4" for nests, 1/2" for outworlds (for all his ants, including *Camponotus*); if only one size, 1/2" OD (that is about 12.7 mm OD, 9.5 mm ID by conversion). Use the smallest size that suits the ants, since wide tubes get packed with soil or brood. [single keeper rptraut, experienced; mixed species] [F] — [Formiculture 20138](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/20138-standard-tubing-size/); [21893](https://formiculture.com/topic/21893-how-to-connect-tubing-to-outworld)
- "A lot of folks use 16mm"; the poster uses 20 mm because it was cheap and came with corks. No standard established. [single keeper Neme123; species not stated] [F] — [Formiculture 20138](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/20138-standard-tubing-size/)
- Vendor sizes: connector for tube 13 mm OD, 10 mm ID ([HighTech Ants](https://www.antkeepingdepot.com/products/hightech-ants-nest-foundation-with-monitor)) [vendor; F]; PVC 12/16 mm ([Ant-Shack](https://www.ant-shack.com/products/pvc-tubing-12-16mm)) [vendor; S, title only]; vinyl 16 mm ([antkeepers](https://antkeepers.com/products/vinyl-tubing-16-mm-0-5-m)) [vendor; S, title only]; vinyl 9.5 and 12.7 mm with cone adapter ([PorAmor](https://www.poramorart.ca/product-page/cone-connector-and-tubing)) [vendor; S]. Species general.
- *L. niger* setup: 8 mm and 16 mm tubing into Ytong, 16 mm meant for nest access or a test tube; 10 mm bore in the tank. [single keeper; *L. niger*] [F] — [Pierre](https://www.ameisenhaltung.de/cafe/thread/8406-pierre-s-lasius-niger/)
- Silicone sleeves sized for test tubes: 14 mm into 15 mm OD tube, 15 mm into 16 mm OD tube. [vendor; general] [S] — [PorAmor test tube port](https://www.poramorart.ca/product-page/test-tube-port)
- Openings on a nest kit: 14 mm and 27 mm; plugs 13, 27, 50 mm. [vendor; M-L species] [F] — [AntCube](https://antcube.shop/en/product/ytongnest-40x20-with-arena/)

**Drilling, joints and sealing**
- Acrylic: brad-point bit for pilot hole, step drill for a 3/4" hole with a clean edge, mark stopping depth, about one inch above the floor. Connector: a one-inch piece of 3/4" OD tube through the wall; 1/2" tube inserted about 3/4" each end; hot glue to hold, but fit must leave no gap. [single keeper rptraut; mixed species] [F] — [Formiculture 21893](https://formiculture.com/topic/21893-how-to-connect-tubing-to-outworld)
- Friction only: connections held over a year with a tight fit; slight tube angle pinches it; small ring of Blu-Tack if needed. [single keeper Full_Frontal_Yeti; species not stated] [F] — [Formiculture 21893](https://formiculture.com/topic/21893-how-to-connect-tubing-to-outworld)
- Joins nest one size inside the next; moisten the inserted tube; to separate, wet and flex or wiggle. Cut with heavy scissors or a box cutter. [single keeper rptraut] [F] — [20138](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/20138-standard-tubing-size/); [21893](https://formiculture.com/topic/21893-how-to-connect-tubing-to-outworld)
- Test tube to tubing: fold a cotton pad lengthwise, wrap it around the tube end, push into the tube. [vendor; *L. niger*] [F] — [QualityAnts](https://qualityants.nl/en/starting-an-ant-colony-lasius-niger)

**Plugs and valves**
- Unused connectors get rubber stoppers. [single keeper rptraut] [F] — [21893](https://formiculture.com/topic/21893-how-to-connect-tubing-to-outworld)
- To service a wall: plug the nest entrance, aspirate foragers out, clean with alcohol, re-coat, wait about an hour, hold ants in a Fluon-lined box, return when fully dry. [single keeper nurbs, moderator; *Myrmecocystus mexicanus*] [F] — [Formiculture 14382](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14382-what-to-do-when-fluon-starts-failing/)
- Dedicated shut-off valves: no page read describes one in use.

**Opening a new nest to the colony**
- *L. niger* about 15 workers: attached to the formicarium's outer hole, foraged within days, but the colony may stay in the tube "a few weeks longer"; do not force; colonies of hundreds can live in tubes well. [ผู้เลี้ยง-consensus; *L. niger* plus others] [F] — [Formiculture 5250](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/5250-moving-an-ant-colony-from-a-test-tube-set-up-to-formicarium)
- Incentives: new nest dark and moist, old tube bright, warm and dry; or leave the tube uncovered in the outworld to dry. Attach a second dark tube and let the queen move brood. Wait for workers before pushing. [ผู้เลี้ยง-consensus; *T. immigrans*] [F] — [Formiculture 12181](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/12181-please-i-need-help-moving-my-ant-colony/)
- Too much heat next to the old tube can overheat or injure brood; one post suggests heat near the new tube instead. This conflicts with the bright, warm old tube advice above, and in degree with the "dark and cool" new nest advice in [Ant-Shack relocation](https://www.ant-shack.com/blogs/ant-articles/ant-colony-relocation-best-practices-for-a-smooth-transition-1). [single keeper; species not confirmed] [S] — [Formiculture 7357 (results set, exact thread unconfirmed)](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/7357-moving-from-one-testtube-setup-to-another/)
- Replace a dirty tube by attaching a fresh filled tube and waiting. [vendor; general] [S] — [Ant-Shack test tube](https://www.ant-shack.com/de/products/test-tube)
- Timing: "several days or even weeks"; avoid peak brood or recent disturbance. [vendor, generic blog] [F] — [Ant-Shack relocation](https://www.ant-shack.com/blogs/ant-articles/ant-colony-relocation-best-practices-for-a-smooth-transition-1)

**Several nests**
- Tubing and connector ports chained between nest and outworlds; extra feeding boxes added by tube as needed. [single keeper jeoff82; *L. niger* setup] [F] — [antstore thread](https://www.antstore.net/forum/post102393.html)
- A second foraging box attached by tube, with food and water moved over, draws crowding off a wall for barrier work. [single keeper ANTdrew, moderator; species not stated] [F] — [Formiculture 14382](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14382-what-to-do-when-fluon-starts-failing/)

### Inferences
- Games can treat connection as a friction joint with a small "leak" check, and treat the nest opening as a slow colony decision rather than a player action. 1/2" and 16 mm disagree in round numbers but both fit *L. niger* (3-5 mm workers per [QualityAnts](https://qualityants.nl/en/starting-an-ant-colony-lasius-niger)); that fit is inference.

### Gaps
- Rate of drilled hole failures or acrylic cracking: not found. Measured move duration for *L. niger* beyond "weeks": not found. Evidence on tube-length limits: not found.

## Escape barriers: Fluon/PTFE, talc, oils, Vaseline, moats, lids, mesh

### Takeaway
Fluon is the standard but fails with humidity; the fine talc-plus-alcohol mix has the only controlled evidence (not on *L. niger*); oils are cheap and need renewal and can kill. Durability claims disagree by a factor of ten.

### Cited findings

**Fluon/PTFE**
- Apply: gently stir (do not shake), cotton swab, one coat, band 1/2-1 inch on upper inner edge, dry to whitish film, re-do after cleaning with rubbing alcohol. Turn the bottle over every few weeks. Ventilated area; toxic fumes if burned. No drying time, shelf life or humidity warning given. [vendor; general] [F] — [AntGear Fluon](https://antgear.com/?p=510)
- Disagreement on thickness: "a thin line", dry until slightly white ([QualityAnts](https://qualityants.nl/en/starting-an-ant-colony-lasius-niger), vendor, *L. niger*) vs "thick barrier" ([ANTdrew, 14693](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14693-best-way-to-contain-small-ants-like-tetramorium/)) vs an even coat on inside and outside ([drtrmiller, 14693](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14693-best-way-to-contain-small-ants-like-tetramorium/), vendor).
- Disagreement on life: over two years, one application, on an upside-down lip of an open-top acrylic area, large *Solenopsis* ([drtrmiller, 5405](https://formiculture.com/topic/5405-barrier-question), vendor) vs a few months, renewed about every two months ([ANTdrew, 14382](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14382-what-to-do-when-fluon-starts-failing/); [14693](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14693-best-way-to-contain-small-ants-like-tetramorium/), keeper, *Tetramorium* and others) [F].
- Humidity failure: the surfactant is hygroscopic and pulls water from air, and condensation forms on treated areas; misted terrarium conditions make it useless. Water contact is not the main problem; relative humidity is. Advises an open, relatively dry enclosure and small-scale tests first. [vendor drtrmiller; species not stated] [F] — [Formiculture 6468](https://formiculture.com/topic/6468-is-byformica-fluon-water-resistant)
- Observed failure at about 90% RH in a garage. [single keeper AntsMaryland; species not stated] [F] — [Formiculture 6620](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/6620-what-barriers-do-i-use-for-high-humidity/)
- Observed failure in colonies of several hundred: workers repeatedly climb the same spots day and night until the band gives out. [single keeper OhNoNotAgain; species not stated in that thread] [F] — [Formiculture 14382](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14382-what-to-do-when-fluon-starts-failing/)
- Dust and contamination degrade it; surfaces need a clean film; silicone seams need adhesive tape first. [encyclopaedia/vendor; general] [F] — [dewiki](https://dewiki.de/Lexikon/Formicarium); [AntGear](https://antgear.com/?p=510)
- Removal differs: damp paper towel or microfibre (ANTdrew) vs scraping (ponerinecat) vs rubbing alcohol (nurbs). [single keepers each] [F] — [Formiculture 14382](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14382-what-to-do-when-fluon-starts-failing/)

**Talc with alcohol**
- Ning, Yang, Xiao, Ran and Xu, *Myrmecological News*. Talc plus ethanol stopped 100% of *S. invicta* at "20%" (the blog does not make clear of what) and 100% of *Monomorium intrudens* with D97 of 2-3 µm, and above 1% talc at 2 µm. Painted on plastic boxes, Petri dishes and shelves. Effect more than three months, stable for one year. Plain talc falls off and harms ants. Authors call it more efficient than Fluon for small species, cheaper and easier to clean; no numeric comparison. [peer-reviewed, read through author interview on the journal blog, paper itself not read; not *L. niger*] [F] — [Myrmecological News blog](https://blog.myrmecologicalnews.org/2019/04/10/talc-powder-ethanol-a-simple-and-efficient-method-for-preventing-ants-from-escaping/)
- Commenter on that blog says French myrmecologists have used it for decades. [single keeper/researcher] [F] — same URL
- Water paste variant: brush on, dry 1-2 days, renew about once a year; roughen with a finger or fine brush for very small species such as *Pheidole pallidula*. [encyclopaedia; general] [F] — [dewiki](https://dewiki.de/Lexikon/Formicarium)
- Baby powder: often 30-44 µm, crumbles, or is now cornstarch. Spread thickly. Coarser grades are fine for larger species. [single keepers katzenhai2, 100lols; general] [F] — [Formiculture 20444](https://formiculture.com/topic/20444-whats-a-good-escape-barrier-other-then-fluon)
- Baby powder plus alcohol results disagree: "works fine" but messy to reapply at about 90% RH ([6620](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/6620-what-barriers-do-i-use-for-high-humidity/)); *Tetramorium* got through ([14693](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14693-best-way-to-contain-small-ants-like-tetramorium/)); less effective for AntsCali098 ([20444](https://formiculture.com/topic/20444-whats-a-good-escape-barrier-other-then-fluon)). Not enough for large *Solenopsis* colonies ([5405](https://formiculture.com/topic/5405-barrier-question), vendor) [F].

**Paraffin oil**
- Band on inner edge about 3x ant length, no drips, on smooth glass or plastic; renew every 2-4 months; large drops can suffocate ants; large colonies smear it, so clean the area. 10 ml covers roughly 10-15 m of a 2 cm band. [vendor; general] [F] — [antstore oil](https://www.antstore.net/shop/en/components/escape-protection/ant-escape-protection-oil-10ml-586.html); [AntCube oil](https://antcube.shop/en/?p=2820)
- "Many ants die" because oil clogs tracheae. Disagrees in degree with the vendor framing. [encyclopaedia; general] [F] — [dewiki](https://dewiki.de/Lexikon/Formicarium)
- Used with a frame in a *L. niger* tank. [single keeper; *L. niger*] [F] — [Pierre](https://www.ameisenhaltung.de/cafe/thread/8406-pierre-s-lasius-niger/)
- Ants pile trash, cotton and dead workers on oil and cross it. [ผู้เลี้ยง-consensus; *Iridomyrmex*, *Tetramorium*] [F] — [14382](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14382-what-to-do-when-fluon-starts-failing/); [14693](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14693-best-way-to-contain-small-ants-like-tetramorium/)

**Olive oil** (disagreement)
- 2" band on glass and plastic lasted 3-4 months, lidless; ants eat it, so reapply ([Jefw1f](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14693-best-way-to-contain-small-ants-like-tetramorium/), *Tetramorium*). Reapply every 2-3 months ([antsriondel](https://formiculture.com/topic/20444-whats-a-good-escape-barrier-other-then-fluon)). Beads up within a day or so, runs and pools ([klawfran3](https://formiculture.com/topic/5405-barrier-question)); forms droplets ants drown in ([20444](https://formiculture.com/topic/20444-whats-a-good-escape-barrier-other-then-fluon)); "no match for Fluon" ([ANTdrew](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14693-best-way-to-contain-small-ants-like-tetramorium/)). [single keepers each] [F]

**Vaseline** (disagreement)
- Works for most small species ([AntsCali098](https://formiculture.com/topic/20444-whats-a-good-escape-barrier-other-then-fluon)); did not work for most of Virginian_ants' colonies; very hard to clean ([20444](https://formiculture.com/topic/20444-whats-a-good-escape-barrier-other-then-fluon)). **Layering**
- Formicarium owners often use two or more safeguards. [encyclopaedia; general] [S] — [Wikipedia Ant Farm (results set, exact page unconfirmed)](https://Www.Wikipedia.org/wiki/Ant_Farm)

### Inferences
- The two-year Fluon claim and the two-month claim may both be true: one is an open, dry, inverted-lip setup, and the other a crowded, climbing colony. This is my reading; no source tests it.
- Game-relevant model: barrier strength should decay faster with humidity, traffic and dust, and bridges (plant, debris, touching objects) should bypass it entirely.

### Gaps
- Measured Fluon life or *L. niger* barrier trials: not found. Ning paper's full methods not read. Talc-to-alcohol ratio for keepers: only "20%" and ">1%" found, poorly defined. Mesh aperture: not found.

## Feeding and water equipment

### Takeaway
Cotton-stoppered tubes and purpose-made feeders are the standard; the failures are leaks that flood, cotton that moulds in days, and open droplets that drown. Small servings and removal of leftovers are the usual prevention.

### Cited findings

**Water stations**
- Fill a tube 1/4 to 1/3 with water, push cotton up to the water so it soaks and seals. [encyclopaedia; general] [F] — [dewiki](https://dewiki.de/Lexikon/Formicarium)
- Tube size: 16 x 150 mm ([QualityAnts](https://qualityants.nl/en/starting-an-ant-colony-lasius-niger), vendor, *L. niger*, F); about 13 x 2 cm ([results set, one guide, S](https://test-tube-formicarium.pages.dev/)). Attribution of the second is unconfirmed.
- Pill container packed with soaked cotton, no reservoir; replaced when dry and ants remove it. Also energy drink cans filled with cotton or paper towel. [single keeper dermy; species not stated] [F] — [Formiculture 8955](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/8955-how-to-water-your-ants/)
- A tube water station doubles as spare nesting space and "lasts ages"; feeders need refilling every 1-2 weeks. [single keeper CoolColJ; species not stated] [F] — [8955](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/8955-how-to-water-your-ants/)
- Poll, 14 votes: 42.86% bought a feeder, 28.57% cotton-plugged tube, 21.43% homemade, 7.14% other. Small, informal sample. [forum poll] [F] — [8955](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/8955-how-to-water-your-ants/)

## Climate: heating, thermostats, probes, power safety, gauges, misting, hibernation hardware

### Takeaway
Most sources say heat only part of the setup under a thermostat, and that *L. niger* does not strictly need heat at room temperature. For winter, keepers use 2-12°C options, mainly fridge or wine cooler, for 3-5 months; the one peer-reviewed *L. niger* result shows +2°C harmed workers versus +7-8°C, though not queens.

### Cited findings

**Do you need heat, and what temperature**
- Heat not needed at room temperature above 15°C; range 17-28°C, optimal 23-26°C. [vendor; *L. niger*] [F] — [QualityAnts](https://qualityants.nl/en/starting-an-ant-colony-lasius-niger)
- Nest 20-27°C; heat around 25°C for a couple of hours a day (hedged); heat one part only. [vendor; *L. niger*] [F] — [antkeepers care sheet](https://antkeepers.com/pages/care-sheet-lasius-niger)
- A keeper sets a mat at 75°F continuously outside hibernation. [single keeper prettycode; species not stated] [F] — [Formiculture 3020](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/3020-questions-on-heaters)
- *L. niger* brood needs 20°C or above. [single keeper] [S] — [antstore results set](https://www.antstore.net/forum/post36775.html); exact post unconfirmed.

**Cable, mat or lamp** (disagreement)
- Cable preferred: radiant, needs no contact, height adjustable. Mat must touch the object. [single keeper-vendor AntsDavey; general] [F] — [AntsDavey heating](https://www.antsdavey.co.uk/antkeepingguide-heating)
- Cable rather than mat for a gradient; only very low wattage; standoffs supplied. [vendor; L. niger cited as an example] [F] — [HighTech Ants](https://www.antkeepingdepot.com/products/hightech-ants-nest-foundation-with-monitor)
- 15-25 W cable for medium-to-large formicaria; lamp for small setups if light misses the queen; heat walls or ceilings rather than under the nest. Disagrees with placing under the nest. [single keeper iXvXi; general] [F] — [3020](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/3020-questions-on-heaters)
- Mat with thermostat has been reliable; black mats hide escaped ants, so white paper below. [single keeper prettycode] [F] — [3020](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/3020-questions-on-heaters)
- Plug-in dimmer, about $10, adjusts a pad or cable. [single keeper Runner12] [F] — [3020](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/3020-questions-on-heaters)
- Lamps with night bulbs for desert species, nest tops 87-110°F. Not applicable to *L. niger*. [single keeper NightsWebs; desert species] [F] — [3020](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/3020-questions-on-heaters)

**Placement**
- Raise the tube or nest above the cable; snake under and secure; more wraps give more heat. Heat at most half the tube, on the side away from the water reservoir. Avoid hydration ports (condensation). Vertical pods may need a mat taped to the side. Outworld heating optional. Set up heating before moving ants in. [single keeper-vendor AntsDavey; general] [F] — [AntsDavey](https://www.antsdavey.co.uk/antkeepingguide-heating)
- Heat one side, do not wrap fully, and never bury a cable. [vendor AntsCanada; general] [S, page 403] — [AC cable manual](https://manuals.online/antscanada/cables-and-connectors/achc/user-manual/1396l)
- Avoid heat mats on test tubes as a beginner. [vendor; *L. niger*] [F] — [QualityAnts](https://qualityants.nl/en/starting-an-ant-colony-lasius-niger)

**Thermostat and probe**
- Probe a few mm from the heat source, not on it, firmly secured; a fallen probe can overheat ants. Pulse-proportional is best for precision; on/off dial is the budget option. Dummy tube at the same height with a waterproof probe to read test tube conditions. For mats, probe inside the box with the tube. [single keeper-vendor AntsDavey] [F] — [AntsDavey](https://www.antsdavey.co.uk/antkeepingguide-heating)
- Probe buried in sand in a weighted acrylic box beside the nests, to imitate nest conditions. [single keeper prettycode] [F] — [3020](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/3020-questions-on-heaters)
- Disagreement: reptile guides put the probe at animal level on the floor above the mat, not on the mat, since on the mat it regulates to the hottest point. Ant guide puts it near the source. [reptile keeper/guide, not ants] [S] — [furrycritter on/off thermostats](https://www.furrycritter.com/pages/articles/reptiles/on_off_thermostats.htm)
- Failures: thermostats can fail on or off; probes shift; the serious case is a dual failure; a second thermostat that fails by cutting power is one keeper's suggestion. [reptile forum; not ants] [S] — [ball-pythons.net](https://ball-pythons.net/forums/showthread.php?p=2781509)
- Thermostats run at or above their rating and mats trapped with no air gap are fire risks. [reptile guide; not ants] [S] — [furrycritter heat source safety](https://www.furrycritter.com/pages/articles/snakes/heat_source_safety.htm). Exact page in this results set unconfirmed.

**Gauges**

**Moisture and misting**
- Moisture given by spraying. [vendor; *L. niger*] [F] — [antkeepers](https://antkeepers.com/pages/care-sheet-lasius-niger)
- Add a few drops to a plaster piece, watch the monitor. [vendor] [F] — [HighTech](https://www.antkeepingdepot.com/products/hightech-ants-nest-foundation-with-monitor)
- Hourly misting asked about for a terrarium; vendor says it destroys Fluon. [vendor; species not stated] [F] — [6468](https://formiculture.com/topic/6468-is-byformica-fluon-water-resistant)

**Hibernation: peer-reviewed**

[peer-reviewed; *L. niger*] [S, abstract via index; repository blocked] — [Turku record](https://research.utu.fi/converis/portal/detail/Publication/3223334?lang=en_US)

[F all]

### Inferences
- The evidence favours about 5-8°C as a safe target for *L. niger*: above the +2°C worker harm in Haatanen et al, below the 12°C where keepers see foraging. This is an inference across different kinds of evidence.
- Reasonable game hazards: drying in the cooler, power cut warming, and vent cold spots. Each is one or two anecdotes, so scale them mildly.
- Heat-gradient design ("heat half, let ants choose") is the most repeated hardware rule and fits a gameplay system well.

### Gaps
- Step-down rate: not found. Wattage per nest size: only 15-25 W from one keeper. Fail-state behaviour of specific thermostats: not found for ant keeping. Wine cooler versus fridge survival data: not found. Mains-safety standard for ant heaters: not found. A fridge drawbacks excerpt (vibration, light, condensation, moisture loss) was seen in search but could not be tied to a page, so it is unverified. Cellar and misting evidence for *L. niger*: thin.

## Handling tools and moving colonies

### Takeaway
A simple kit of aspirator, paint brush, syringe, tweezers and a card covers most work. Moves are normally voluntary via attached tubes; forced moves such as tapping or dumping risk brood.

### Cited findings

[single keeper] [F] — [16889](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/16889-aspirator-operation-tips/); [22319](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/22319-so-im-being-told-aspiratorspooters-kill-queens-and-stress-then-out-too-much-to-use)

## Full installation walk-through, first purchase to running colony

### Takeaway
No source read gives one complete timed sequence. The order below is my assembly from pieces, with each step's support marked; timings are only given where a source stated one.

### Cited findings (facts that the order rests on)
- Kit contents for queen plus first workers: tube outworld, 16 x 150 mm spare tubes, cotton, Fluon, syringe, sugar water, honeydew, fruit flies. [vendor; *L. niger*] [F] — [QualityAnts](https://qualityants.nl/en/starting-an-ant-colony-lasius-niger)

- Water tube: fill 1/4-1/3, push cotton. [encyclopaedia] [F] — [dewiki](https://dewiki.de/Lexikon/Formicarium)
- Barrier must dry before ants connect; "slightly white". [vendor; *L. niger*] [F] — [QualityAnts](https://qualityants.nl/en/starting-an-ant-colony-lasius-niger). Fluon about an hour ([nurbs](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/14382-what-to-do-when-fluon-starts-failing/), single keeper). Talc paste 1-2 days ([dewiki](https://dewiki.de/Lexikon/Formicarium)). [F]
- Set up heat before moving ants in; verify with a dummy tube. [single keeper-vendor] [F] — [AntsDavey](https://www.antsdavey.co.uk/antkeepingguide-heating)
- Test barriers on a small scale first. [vendor drtrmiller] [F] — [6468](https://formiculture.com/topic/6468-is-byformica-fluon-water-resistant)

- Start small: test tube with first workers; straw hole in the cotton once workers appear. [single keeper] [S] — [greaterwrong](https://www.greaterwrong.com/posts/NBqhWBdagH8QfeEfo/keeping-ants-and-spotting-queens)
- Moving takes days to weeks, *L. niger* can be stubborn. [F] — [5250](https://www.formiculture.com/topic/5250-moving-an-ant-colony-from-a-test-tube-set-up-to-formicarium)

### Inferences (assembled sequence; order is inference, step content is cited above)
1. Founding stage: queen in a tube, kept dark and undisturbed until workers appear. No hardware needed. Duration: not covered in this research; see the separate husbandry notes.
2. Buy the arena, tube, plugs, barrier, feeder and spare water tube. Time not found.
3. Dry-fit: drill or check ports, push tubes for a tight fit, stopper unused ports. Time not found; this is minutes of work by inference.
4. Clean and coat the arena's upper wall; Fluon about 1 hour to dry, talc paste 1-2 days. Test on a small piece if humidity is high.
5. Add dry bare or thin (under about 25 mm) substrate. Fill water tube and feeder. Remove anything touching the walls.
6. If heating, run the cable or mat under part of the nest, thermostat and probe secured, and leave it running with a dummy tube until stable. A soak time was not found; I would allow at least a day, but that is my own guess, not sourced.
7. Pre-add checklist, assembled by me from the failures above: no gap at joints, no bridging object, barrier dry, water tube not leaking, probe fixed, temperature read at ant level.
8. Connect the colony's tube and wait. Feed in the arena in small doses. Expect days before foraging and weeks before a full move.
9. Weekly, then seasonally: refill, remove leftovers, renew barrier every 2-4 months (oil) or when ants climb (Fluon), and prepare for winter: the only dated schedule read is the vendor's start in early December with a step-down over a few weeks, held until March; another vendor says October to around March.

- A source-backed start-to-finish sequence with times: not found. Formal leak or pressure test for tubes or a burn-in time for heaters: not found. Vendor manuals with step lists were mostly blocked or absent.

## Upgrade path at 1, 10, 100, 1,000+ workers

### Takeaway
No source sets a tier table. What can be cited is that *L. niger* can stay in tubes for a long time, about 100 workers is a reported move point, and cramped tubes are the vendor's trigger. The table below is inference, not a source claim.

### Cited findings
- Move when the tube is very crowded and maximum space has been given. [vendor; *L. niger*] [F] — [QualityAnts](https://qualityants.nl/en/starting-an-ant-colony-lasius-niger)
